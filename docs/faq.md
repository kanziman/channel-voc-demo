# Help Center — Frequently Asked Questions

_Maintained by the Channel VOC Intelligence Dept. Growth-Ops arm._

## Orders

**How do I track my order?**
You can track your order from **My Page → Orders**. A tracking number appears once
the parcel is handed to the carrier.

## Payments

**Which payment methods are supported?**
We support major credit/debit cards and local wallets at checkout.

## Refunds

**How long does a refund take?**
Refunds are processed within a few business days after approval.
